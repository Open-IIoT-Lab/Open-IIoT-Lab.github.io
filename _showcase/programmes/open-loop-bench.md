    <h3>Open Loop Bench</h3>
    <p><strong>Status:</strong> instrumenting · <strong>Started:</strong> 2025</p>
    <p>
        A scenario harness for testing industrial AI before it meets a production line. Scenarios
        are replayable traces with declared deadlines, so a new model can be compared against an old
        one honestly — including the runs that were late.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Deterministic replay of recorded plant traces (v0.2).</li>
        <li>Deadline accounting instead of mean latency.</li>
        <li>Input hashes, so a rerun that is not the same rerun is visible.</li>
    </ul>
