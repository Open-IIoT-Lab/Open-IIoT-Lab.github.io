About the lab

The Open Industrial IoT Lab is led by Chaojie Gu at the College of Control Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University. We develop networking and learning methods for connected industrial systems, with an emphasis on reliable communication, meaningful data and timely decisions.

Our research brings together models, algorithms and working prototypes, from wireless platforms and deterministic networks to industrial information modeling and edge intelligence. We share research tools and implementations to support further exploration.

Affiliated with NeSC (Group of Networked Sensing and Control).
