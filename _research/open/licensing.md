<div class="p-4">
    <h3>Licensing and reuse</h3>
    <p>
        Lab reports, reference implementations, and field notes are published so that they can be
        reused, including commercially. Reuse is the point; attribution is how the work stays
        traceable back to the plant that taught us something.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>Reports and notes: cite the report identifier (for example <code>OIL-TR-2025-02</code>).</li>
        <li>Reference implementations: released under the licence stated in each programme.</li>
        <li>Data: published only where the plant has agreed, and never in a form that identifies a customer's process.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>
        This website itself is built on an MIT-licensed template; the artwork and font credits are
        in the footer, and the licence file ships with the repository.
    </p>
</div>
