<div class="p-4">
    <h2>Working in the open</h2>
    <hr />
    <p>
        We publish early and version honestly. A reference architecture with an unresolved-problems
        section is more useful to a plant than a polished diagram that hides its assumptions, and it
        is easier for somebody else to disagree with.
    </p>
    <p>
        In practice that means three habits: every programme carries the reports it produced, every
        report says who can be wrong about it, and every result is published in a form that a third
        party can reproduce — including the runs that were late.
    </p>
    <p>
        The cards below describe how to work with the lab, and how our output may be reused. If you
        would rather start from the research itself, the
        <a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publication list</a> is the shortest route, and
        the <a href="{{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}">contact page</a> says how to reach us.
    </p>
</div>
