    <h3>Plant Graph</h3>
    <p><strong>Status:</strong> field study · <strong>Started:</strong> 2024</p>
    <p>
        An operational knowledge graph that links assets, signals, procedures, and people into one
        reviewable digital thread. The graph is only useful if a maintenance engineer can disagree
        with it, so every modelling decision is written down next to the thing it describes.
    </p>
    <ul>
        <li>1,000 modelled assets from a live production line.</li>
        <li>Mapping tables published with the mappings, unknown values kept distinct from empty ones.</li>
        <li>A review workflow that runs before a mapping reaches production.</li>
    </ul>
