Principal investigator

Chaojie Gu

Chaojie Gu is a ZJU 100 Young Professor and doctoral supervisor at the College of Control Science and Engineering, Zhejiang University, and Deputy Director of the Institute of Industrial Process Control. He received his B.Eng. from Harbin Institute of Technology and his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

His research includes Industrial IoT, low-power wide-area networks, deterministic networking and mobile computing. He is a Senior Member of IEEE and CCF and serves as an Associate Editor for IEEE Transactions on Industrial Informatics, IEEE Internet of Things Journal and Smart Health.

His recognitions include the NSFC Excellent Young Scientists Fund (Overseas) and the IEEE TCSC Award for Excellence (Early Career Researcher).
