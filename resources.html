Resources

Open-source software from the lab: the runtimes, toolkits, and harnesses our programmes are built on. They are meant to be read and run by somebody else — small enough to understand in an afternoon, and published with the reports that explain where they fail.

Everything is developed in the open under the lab's GitHub organization: github.com/{{ site.data.profile.github }}.
