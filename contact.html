Contact

We are open to conversations that start with a real constraint rather than a pitch: a line that will not hold a deadline, a protocol nobody documented, a model that behaves differently on the night shift than it did in the lab.

Join us

We have openings for motivated Master and PhD students and for postdocs, and we host undergraduate researchers and visiting engineers. The work sits in Internet of Things systems — industrial IoT, low-power wide-area networks, deterministic networking, and mobile computing.

What we look for is not a long publication list but evidence that you finish things and can explain what went wrong:

  • Something you built, broke, and fixed — hardware, firmware, software, or a measurement setup.
  • A problem you can describe in one page, with the constraint that makes it hard.
  • Willingness to write down what you observed before you decide what it means.

Write to the lab lead, Chaojie Gu (中文主页), with your CV and transcripts. Tell us what you want to work on, what you have already tried, and which of our programmes or reports you read on the way here — that last part is the most useful thing you can put in a first message.

Collaborate with us

Plants, integrators, and research groups: we take on bounded problems where a result can be measured and published. Bring the constraints, the signals you already have, and a definition of "better" — we will tell you honestly if it is not a fit.

We also like it when people run our work and tell us where it broke. A defect report against one of our reference implementations is a genuine contribution.
