Research

From physical signals to dependable industrial decisions

Industrial intelligence starts with a measurement. That measurement must be trustworthy, reach the right place on time, and make sense to the system that uses it. We study the connections between these requirements, from radio waveforms and network protocols to information models and learning.

Our work combines models, algorithms and working systems. Four connected questions guide it.
