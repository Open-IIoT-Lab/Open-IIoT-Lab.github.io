Research

Connect the physical world. Make its data useful.

Our research follows two connected directions. In Industrial Networks, we make information available reliably and efficiently. In Industrial Intelligence, we turn that information into models, perception and decisions.

The two meet in working systems: what a machine can learn depends on what it can sense and communicate, while application needs shape how networks and computing resources should be designed.
