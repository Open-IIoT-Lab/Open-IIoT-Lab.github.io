Research

The group's two broad directions are industrial networks and industrial intelligence, as listed on Chaojie Gu's Zhejiang University profile. The topics below connect those directions to work documented in the publication list.

Industrial networks

Industrial IoT and interoperability

Industrial communication and information models that help heterogeneous equipment and systems exchange data.

Related work: Text2UA; resilience and interoperability in the industrial internet.

Deterministic and time-sensitive networking

Routing, scheduling and reliability for networks with timing requirements, including TSN and TSN–Wi-Fi.

Related work: DARS; reliability-aware TSN–Wi-Fi scheduling; large-scale deterministic networks.

Low-power wide-area networks

LoRa and NB-IoT communication, radio implementations, network performance and security.

Related work: SoftNB; LoPhy.

Industrial intelligence

Intelligent production systems

Learning-based scheduling and information modeling for manufacturing systems.

Related work: flexible job shop scheduling; Text2UA.

Mobile and edge computing

Connected devices, mobile systems and computation near data sources. This is also an area named on the PI's English homepage.
