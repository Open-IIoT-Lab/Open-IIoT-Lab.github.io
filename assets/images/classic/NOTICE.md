# Windows 98 icons

User-selected collection: Windows 98 Icon Viewer, hosted by Alex Meub.
https://win98icons.alexmeub.com/

Twelve unmodified PNG files are bundled:

- `icons/32/` — five native 32x32 files provide the section-heading icons, displayed at
  their original size.
- `icons/16/` — seven native 16x16 files provide the contact and profile icons, displayed
  at their original size (the People and Contact headings show theirs at 2x, which the
  theme renders with `image-rendering: pixelated`).

The files were extracted unchanged from the collection's official "Download All Icons"
ZIP; they were not resized or converted from larger artwork. Source URLs, archive
entries, intrinsic dimensions, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in manifest.json. The
numeric filename suffix does not identify pixel size.

The collection was explicitly selected by the user for this theme. No explicit artwork
reuse license was found on the viewer page; no MIT or other open-source artwork license is
claimed here. Credit the collection and retain these provenance notes with redistributed
resources.
